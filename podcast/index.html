Behind the Mic

A talk show with a mane budget.

I get to ask the second question.

Jazz Hooves produces and co-hosts. I bring the questions. The guests range from royalty to crafters to whichever critter wanders into the studio.

We talk about big changes, brave choices, friendship, and the work of being yourself in public.

On the Feed

Eight episodes to start with.

Eight episodes from the official feed. Each one opens on Acast.

  1. 01 Meet the Alicorn!Listen With Sunny Starscout · 13:48 Sunny joins me and Jazz for the first episode to talk about how life changed after magic came back — alicorn wings included.
  2. 02 A CrafternoonListen With Izzy Moonbow · 13:23 Izzy brings the crafternoon: unicycles, making things, and why living lighter on the planet is worth the effort.

  5. 05 All Hail the QueenListen With Queen Haven · 14:23 Mom stops by to talk family, forgiveness, and having each other's backs.
  6. 06 A Nightmare Night Monster Party!Listen Pipp and friends · 14:35 The Pippcast turns into a spooky variety show with my friends for my absolute favorite holiday.
  7. 07 Change is GoodListen With Posey · 14:22 Posey makes a surprise appearance to talk about how scary and wonderful change can be — and shows off her new Earth Pony magic.
