Behind the Mic

I ask the questions. Jazz makes it sound planned.

Jazz Hooves produces and co-hosts. I bring the questions, the warm-up, and the occasional unscheduled tangent.

The guest brings the story. I bring the follow-up that makes them laugh, then Jazz edits it into something that sounds like I planned it.

On the Feed

Eight episodes. Start with Mom.

Each episode opens on Acast.

  1. 01 Meet the Alicorn!Listen With Sunny Starscout · 13:48 Sunny joins me and Jazz for the first episode to talk about how her life changed overnight, wings included.
  2. 02 A CrafternoonListen With Izzy Moonbow · 13:23 Izzy brings the crafternoon: unicycles, making things, and why living lighter on the planet is worth the effort.
  3. 03 Yours Truly With Pipp PetalsListen Jazz turns the mic around · 14:22 Jazz flips the script and interviews me about helping ponies find their inner sparkle. I talked a lot. Shocking.
  4. 04 It's Critter Time!Listen With Hitch Trailblazer · 13:47 Hitch on becoming a dragon dad overnight and balancing sheriff duty with parenting a very small, very fiery critter.
  5. 05 A Nightmare Night Monster Party!Listen Pipp and friends · 14:35 The Pippcast turns into a spooky variety show with my friends for my absolute favorite holiday.
  6. 06 All Hail the QueenListen With Queen Haven · 14:23 Mom stops by to talk family, forgiveness, and having each other's backs.
  7. 07 Change is GoodListen With Posey · 14:22 Posey makes a surprise appearance to talk about how scary and wonderful change can be, and shows off a new trick.
  8. 08 FanponiesListen With Glory, Seashell and Peach Fizz · 14:37 Seashell, Glory, and Peach Fizz stop by to talk about how the Pippsqueaks turned them into best friends.
