The Small Print

Art and music credits.

This is a non-commercial fan project. My Little Pony, Pipp Petals and all related marks belong to Hasbro and their respective rights holders. No endorsement or affiliation is implied.

Character and location art

Pipp character art and the Pippcast cover were supplied by the site editor. Original materials are credited to Hasbro.

Zephyr Heights, the Crystal Brighthouse, Pipp's bedroom, the Mane Melody stage and salon, and the royal family portrait are official Generation 5 assets archived on Derpibooru:

  • Zephyr Heights: image 3688286
  • Crystal Brighthouse: image 3688300
  • Pipp's bedroom: image 3626705
  • Mane Melody stage: image 3688287
  • Mane Melody interior: image 3706067
  • Royal family portrait: image 3688123

The Pipp cutie mark is an official visual asset. A local copy is used for the header, footer and favicon instead of hotlinking the archive.

Music

Album and single covers are local WebP copies of the publicly available Apple Music artwork, used to identify each release. Music is not mirrored by this site. The 30-second previews on the music page stream directly from Apple's public preview CDN when you press play. No audio file is hosted here.

Release metadata and performer credits were collected from the Apple Music and iTunes Search API in November 2026.

Images and processing

Still images were upscaled with the open-source Real-ESRGAN model and re-encoded to WebP. The location and family assets above were already high resolution, so they were downscaled with Lanczos resampling and converted to WebP without upscaling.

Text

Pipp's pull quotes are short lines of dialogue from the series, attributed to Pipp Petals. They are used as in-character quotations, without episode labels.

Interface components

The lookbook rail uses Embla Carousel (MIT). The image lightbox uses PhotoSwipe (MIT). Typefaces are self-hosted through Fontsource under the SIL Open Font License.
