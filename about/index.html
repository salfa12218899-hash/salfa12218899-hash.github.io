About Pipp Petals

Princess.
Pop star.
Salon owner.

Officially, I'm Princess Pipp Petals of Zephyr Heights. Philomena Petals is the full name, but only the stationery uses it. Pop star, salon owner, and the pony whose sparkle is confidence, which is a lot to live up to before my mane's done.

The Pipp File
The Palace Years

The palace was my first stage.

I grew up in the Royal Palace with Mom and Zipp. Gold trim, tall windows, and a stage where a throne room should be.

Zipp got her cutie mark first. She had been trying to work out how pegasi could fly again, and she impressed a crowd with a parkour run. I watched her and got the nerve to sing in front of everypony for the first time. My mark appeared right after. The crowd started chanting “Pipp, Pipp, hooray!” I kept the chant.

I grew up inside the show: the galas, the cameras, the perfect entrance. Mom is the reason I know how to make one. When magic came back, I could finally fly. Actually fly.

I moved to Maretime Bay, into the Crystal Brighthouse with Sunny, Izzy, Zipp, and eventually Misty. I'm the smallest of the group. I still make the biggest entrance.

The Pipp File

Small pony. Loud entrance.

I'm the younger one, the loud one, and the one who will ask what you're listening to before I touch your mane.

My sparkle is confidence. That's not the same as never being scared. It's what I do right after. I live at the Crystal Brighthouse, I own Mane Melody, and I still check the schedule twice. The Pippsqueaks keep me posting.

The official card rates my kindness and creativity high. Courage is still charging. It also says my hidden talent is finding the perfect lighting. I've never felt so seen.

Profile details come from the official character card. Notes and sources.

The Salon

The best seat in Maretime Bay.

Mane Melody is a salon with a karaoke stage in the back. Manes, hooves, and a mic for anypony who wants one.

I run it, and I still take the mic when the room is right. Some ponies come for the mane. Some come for the song. Most come for both.

One client had already closed five salons with a single bad review. I gave her the mane she already had, and she said it was just the way she liked it. The logo is two music notes shaped like the letter M.

The Pippsqueaks

The phone is part of the stage.

I call my fans the Pippsqueaks. My three biggest in Maretime Bay are Glory, Seashell, and Peach Fizz. They will find you.

I post from the salon, the Brighthouse, and the side of a stage. I want every post, every show, and every mane to be perfect. When it isn't, I worry I've let somepony down. I still check the numbers. I'm learning that the one pony who needed the song matters more.

My cutie mark is a deep magenta music note with pale pink note heads and a gold crown inside it. The note is the singing. The crown is the title. Confidence is my sparkle, and it is the nerve to try plus the friends who remind you to.

“My Pippsqueaks can find anything or anypony!”
