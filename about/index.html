The Palace Years

A princess who performed before she could fly.

Pipp grew up in the Royal Palace in Zephyr Heights with Queen Haven and her older sister, Zipp.

The city believed its royals could fly. They could not. After magic disappeared, the court used invisible wires, careful lighting and a hidden rig room above the throne room to keep the show going.

Pipp played along. She livestreamed the Royal Gala, premiered "Glowin' Up" from the throne room, and ended the night hanging from the rigging. Then she left to help bring magic back.

Magic returned. Pipp could finally fly. She moved to Maretime Bay and into the Crystal Brighthouse with Sunny, Izzy, Zipp and later Misty.

The Salon

The best seat in Maretime Bay.

Mane Melody is white brick outside, pink walls and a red carpet inside, with a stage shaped like a giant boombox.

Pipp runs it with Jazz Hooves and Rocky Riff. Jazz sings and looks after hooves. Rocky sings and styles manes. A one-star review nearly closed the shop in its first week.

Pipp sang "Let Out Your Light" and kept the doors open. The logo is two music notes shaped like the letter M.

The Pippsqueaks

The phone is part of the stage.

Pipp calls her fans the Pippsqueaks, and she treats the phone as part of the act.

She posts from the salon, from the Brighthouse, and from the side of a stage. The point is not the follower count. It is the reply from a pony who needed the song that day.

Her cutie mark is a music note with a crown worked into it. Confidence is the word she keeps coming back to.

“My Pippsqueaks can find anything or anypony!”
