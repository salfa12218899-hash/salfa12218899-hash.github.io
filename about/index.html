About Pipp Petals

Princess.
Pop star.
Salon owner.

Philomena “Pipp” Petals is the younger princess of Zephyr Heights, a pop singer, the owner of Mane Melody, and the element of confidence in the G5 story. She has been a princess, a pop star, and a small-business owner — usually all on the same day.

The Palace Years

A princess who performed before she could fly.

Pipp grew up in the Royal Palace in Zephyr Heights with Queen Haven and her older sister, Zipp.

For years, Zephyr Heights believed its royal family could fly. The truth was more complicated. The palace used wires, careful lighting, and a hidden rig room to keep the illusion going.

Pipp grew up inside that performance — the galas, the cameras, the perfect entrance. When the secret came apart, she joined Zipp and her new friends to bring magic back. At first it was about salvaging her reputation. It did not stay that way.

Magic returned. Pipp could finally fly. She moved to Maretime Bay and into the Crystal Brighthouse with Sunny, Izzy, Zipp, and later Misty. She is the smallest of the group, with the biggest entrance.

The Salon

The best seat in Maretime Bay.

Mane Melody is white brick outside, pink walls and a red carpet inside, with a stage shaped like a giant boombox.

Pipp runs it with Jazz Hooves and Rocky Riff. Between them they handle manes, hooves, and the occasional duet.

Phyllis Cloverleaf had already closed five salons with one bad review. Pipp gave her the mane she already had, and earned five stars instead. The logo is two music notes shaped like the letter M.

The Pippsqueaks

The phone is part of the stage.

Pipp calls her fans the Pippsqueaks, and she treats the phone as part of the act.

She posts from the salon, the Brighthouse, and the side of a stage. She wants every post, every show, and every mane to be perfect. When it isn't, she worries she has let somepony down. She still checks the numbers. She is learning that one pony who needed the song matters more.

Her favorite holiday is Nightmare Night. She will absolutely try to scare you. She also hates being sick, because being sick means sitting still.

Her cutie mark is a music note with a gold crown. Confidence is her sparkle — not a magic spell, but the nerve to try, and the friends who remind you to.

“My Pippsqueaks can find anything or anypony!”
