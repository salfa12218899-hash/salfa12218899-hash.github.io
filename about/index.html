About Pipp Petals

Princess.
Pop star.
Salon owner.

Officially, I'm Princess Pipp Petals of Zephyr Heights. Philomena Petals is the full name, but only the stationery uses it. Pop star, salon owner, and the pony whose sparkle is confidence, which is a lot to live up to before my mane's done.

The Palace Years

I performed before I could fly.

I grew up in the Royal Palace with Mom and Zipp. Gold trim, tall windows, and one family rule: never let them see the wires.

For years, Zephyr Heights believed we could fly. We couldn't. The palace ran on wires, careful lighting, and a control room above the throne. I knew. I played along. I was good at it.

I grew up inside the show: the galas, the cameras, the perfect entrance. Mom is the reason I know how to make one. She is also the reason I know what it costs to keep it going. Then the secret came apart in front of everypony. I joined Zipp and my new friends to bring magic back. At first I was saving my reputation. Somewhere in Bridlewood, it stopped being about that.

Magic came back. I could finally fly. Actually fly. I moved to Maretime Bay, into the Crystal Brighthouse with Sunny, Izzy, Zipp, and eventually Misty. I'm the smallest of the group. I still make the biggest entrance.

The Pipp File

Small pony. Loud entrance.

I'm the younger one, the loud one, and the one who will ask what you're listening to before I touch your mane.

My sparkle is confidence. That's not the same as never being scared. It's what I do right after. I live at the Crystal Brighthouse, I own Mane Melody, and Jazz and Rocky keep me from burning it down. The Pippsqueaks keep me posting.

The official card rates my kindness and creativity high. Courage is still charging. It also says my hidden talent is finding the perfect lighting. I've never felt so seen.

The Salon

The best seat in Maretime Bay.

My salon is white brick outside, pink walls and a red carpet inside, with a stage shaped like a giant boombox.

I run it with Jazz Hooves and Rocky Riff. Jazz handles the hooficures and the harmonies; Rocky does hair. Between us, manes, hooves, and the occasional duet.

Phyllis Cloverleaf had already closed five salons with one bad review. I gave her the mane she already had, and she said it was just the way she liked it. The logo is two music notes shaped like the letter M.

The Pippsqueaks

The phone is part of the stage.

I call my fans the Pippsqueaks. My three biggest in Maretime Bay are Glory, Seashell, and Peach Fizz. They will find you.

I post from the salon, the Brighthouse, and the side of a stage. I want every post, every show, and every mane to be perfect. When it isn't, I worry I've let somepony down. I still check the numbers. I'm learning that the one pony who needed the song matters more.

My favorite holiday is Nightmare Night. I will absolutely try to scare you. I also hate being sick, because being sick means sitting still.

My cutie mark is a music note with a gold crown. Confidence is my sparkle. It is the nerve to try, and the friends who remind you to.

“My Pippsqueaks can find anything or anypony!”
