Where she grew up

Zephyr Heights

A pegasus city above the clouds, a palace built on stagecraft, and a gala that changed everything.

Pipp grew up in the Royal Palace with Queen Haven and her older sister, Zipp.

For years, Zephyr Heights believed its royal family could fly. The truth was more complicated: the palace used wires, careful lighting, and a hidden rig room to keep the illusion going.

Pipp grew up inside that performance. She livestreamed the Royal Gala and premiered "Glowin' Up" from the throne room. When the secret came apart, she left the palace with her sister and helped bring magic back to Equestria.

Where she lives now

Crystal Brighthouse

Sunny's lighthouse came down. The friends rebuilt it around the Unity Crystals, and Pipp moved in.

The tower
A translucent spire with the crystals at the top.
The shared room
Five sections, one big room. Pipp's corner has a crown motif.
The cliff
The Brighthouse stands where Sunny's lighthouse used to.

Pastel blue walls, a turquoise roof, pink trees by the door, and a tower that throws a rainbow beam into the sky.

Sprout's mech tore down Sunny's old lighthouse. The friends rebuilt on the same cliff, around the Unity Crystals. The tower is translucent, and the beam comes from the crystals at the very top.

Inside there is a living room with couches and a fireplace, a kitchen, and curved stairs up to one shared bedroom with five sections. An elevator in the middle goes up to the crystals. Hitch and Sparky keep the sheriff's station, so they visit more than they stay.

One Nightmare Night, Pipp turned the Brighthouse into a haunted house for the whole town.

Where she works

Mane Melody

A mane salon, a karaoke stage, and a logo made of two M-shaped music notes.

White brick outside. Pink walls and a red carpet inside. A stage shaped like a pink and purple boombox with gold speakers.

Pipp runs Mane Melody with Jazz Hooves and Rocky Riff. Between them they handle manes, hooves, and the occasional duet.

Her first big client had a reputation for closing salons with one bad review. Pipp gave her the mane she already had, and earned five stars instead. The logo is two music notes shaped like the letter M.

The Royal Three

Queen Haven, Zipp, and Pipp.

Queen Haven

Queen of Zephyr Heights

Proud and posh, with a weakness for a dramatic entrance. She kept the flight secret to protect Zephyr Heights. Cloudpuff is never far from her side.

Zipp Storm

Older sister, elder princess

A daredevil athlete with a skeptic's streak. Zipp asks the questions the palace would rather ignore. She works as a detective in Maretime Bay and still lives at the Brighthouse.

Pipp Petals

Younger sister, pop star

The performer of the family, and the one who turned a Maretime Bay storefront into a salon with a stage. Her cutie mark is a music note with a gold crown. She is still learning to trust her own voice.

Sisters

Older sister. Younger sister.

Zipp is the older sister and the heir. Pipp is the younger one with the microphone. They tease each other, disagree about almost everything, and show up when it counts.

Zipp asks the difficult question. Pipp makes sure the answer looks good on camera.
