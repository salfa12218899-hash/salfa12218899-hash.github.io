Pipp's World / A Short Tour

The palace,
the lighthouse,
the salon.

Pipp grew up in a cloud palace, moved into a lighthouse full of crystals, and opened a salon with a karaoke stage. This is the short version of how those places shaped her.

Start with Zephyr Heights

Where she grew up

Zephyr Heights

Pipp grew up in the Royal Palace with Queen Haven and her older sister, Zipp.

The throne room doubled as a stage, which is where she learned that an entrance is a promise. Everypony turns, and you have to give them a reason to keep looking.

She premiered “Glowin’ Up” from that stage. The palace is still the family address. She still knows which camera to find.

Hear "Glowin' Up"

Where she lives now

Crystal Brighthouse

Five ponies, one room, and a tower that throws a rainbow beam over Maretime Bay.

Home is a lighthouse in Maretime Bay, and privacy is a group activity.

Pipp films updates from her corner, keeps Bestie within reach, and has learned to check whether anypony is asleep before she starts a livestream.

If somepony is cooking, the whole house knows.

One Nightmare Night, she turned the Brighthouse into a haunted house for the whole town. It was the one night the shared room became a set.

See Pipp's side of Maretime Bay

Who shares the house

Six ponies.

Six ponies, one group chat that never stops.

The house is never quiet for long.

In practice, somepony is always borrowing a charger, somepony is gluing something to the kitchen table, and Pipp is narrating all of it. Zipp is the one who asks whether they should post it.

Where she works

Mane Melody

A mane salon in Maretime Bay with a karaoke stage in the back.

Mane Melody is the salon Pipp runs in Maretime Bay. The chairs face the mirrors. The stage faces the room.

She still takes the mic when the room is right. The playlist is hers, which means the warm-up is longer than the set.

Regulars book a chair and stay for the set. Pipp books the set and stays for the compliments.

The Royal Three

Queen Haven, Zipp, and Pipp.

Queen Haven

Queen of Zephyr Heights

Proud and posh, with a weakness for a dramatic entrance. She is the reason Pipp knows how to make one. Cloudpuff is never far from her side.

Zipp Storm

Older sister, elder princess

A daredevil athlete with a skeptic's streak. Zipp asks the questions the palace would rather ignore. She is the future queen, whether she likes it or not, and works as a detective in Maretime Bay.

Pipp Petals

Younger sister, pop star

The performer of the family, and the one who turned a Maretime Bay storefront into a salon with a stage. Her cutie mark is a music note with a gold crown.

Sisters

Older sister. Younger sister.

They tease each other, disagree about almost everything, and show up when it counts.

Zipp wants the facts. Pipp wants a second take.
