Pipp's World / A Short Tour

The palace,
the lighthouse,
the salon.

Pipp grew up in a cloud palace, moved into a lighthouse full of crystals, and built a salon with a karaoke stage. This is the short version of how those places shaped her.

Start with Zephyr Heights

Where she grew up

Zephyr Heights

A pegasus city above the clouds, the Royal Palace, and the gala where “Glowin’ Up” premiered.

Pipp grew up in the Royal Palace with Queen Haven and her older sister, Zipp.

The throne room doubled as a stage, which is where she learned that an entrance is a promise. Everypony turns, and you have about three seconds to give them a reason.

She livestreamed the Royal Gala from that stage and premiered “Glowin’ Up”. The palace is still the family address. She still knows which camera to find.

Where she lives now

Crystal Brighthouse

One shared room, five corners, and a tower that throws a rainbow beam over Maretime Bay.

Pipp's corner
The pink section with a crown motif, a mirror, and a rug.
The roommates
Sunny, Izzy, Zipp, and Misty. Hitch and Sparky visit more than they stay.

Home is a lighthouse in Maretime Bay, and privacy is a group activity.

Pipp films updates from her corner, keeps Bestie within reach, and has learned to check whether anypony is asleep before she starts a livestream.

The kitchen is communal and the stairs curve up to the shared room. If somepony is cooking, the whole house knows.

One Nightmare Night, she turned the Brighthouse into a haunted house for the whole town. It was the one night the shared room became a set.

Who shares the house

The Mane Six

Sunny, Izzy, Hitch, Zipp, Misty, and Pipp. Six ponies, one Brighthouse, and a group chat that never stops.

The house is never quiet for long.

In practice, somepony is always borrowing a charger, somepony is gluing something to the kitchen table, and Pipp is narrating all of it. Zipp is the one who asks whether they should post it.

The group photo is the easy version. The real version is noisier, with everypony talking at once and Pipp reaching for her phone before anypony finishes a sentence.

Where she works

Mane Melody

A mane salon in Maretime Bay with a karaoke stage in the back.

Mane Melody is the salon Pipp runs in Maretime Bay. The chairs face the mirrors. The stage faces the room.

She still takes the mic when the room is right. The playlist is hers, which means the warm-up is longer than the set.

Regulars book a chair and stay for the set. Pipp books the set and stays for the compliments.

The Royal Three

Queen Haven, Zipp, and Pipp.

Queen Haven

Queen of Zephyr Heights

Proud and posh, with a weakness for a dramatic entrance. She is the reason Pipp knows how to make one. Cloudpuff is never far from her side.

Zipp Storm

Older sister, elder princess

A daredevil athlete with a skeptic's streak. Zipp asks the questions the palace would rather ignore. She is the future queen, whether she likes it or not, and works as a detective in Maretime Bay.

Pipp Petals

Younger sister, pop star

The performer of the family, and the one who turned a Maretime Bay storefront into a salon with a stage. Confidence is her sparkle. Her cutie mark is a music note with a gold crown, and she is still learning to trust her own voice.

Sisters

Older sister. Younger sister.

Zipp is the older sister and the future queen. Pipp is the younger one with the microphone. They tease each other, disagree about almost everything, and show up when it counts.

Zipp wants the facts. Pipp wants a second take. Her hidden talent is finding the perfect lighting.
