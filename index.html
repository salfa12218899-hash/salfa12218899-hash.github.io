Meet Pipp

A pop star with a salon key.

Pipp Petals is the younger princess of Zephyr Heights, a pop star, and the pony behind Mane Melody. She turned a Maretime Bay storefront into a salon with a karaoke stage.

She lives at the Crystal Brighthouse with Sunny, Izzy, Zipp and Misty. Her fans are the Pippsqueaks. Jazz Hooves and Rocky Riff work the salon floor with her.

Maretime Bay

The salon with a stage in it.

Mane Melody is white brick on the outside, pink walls and a red carpet on the inside, and a stage shaped like a giant boombox.

Pipp runs it with Jazz Hooves and Rocky Riff. Book a mane, take the mic, or do both. On a good night the whole room sings the chorus.
