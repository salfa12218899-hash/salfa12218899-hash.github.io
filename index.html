Meet Pipp

Singer, streamer, salon owner.

Pipp Petals is the younger princess of Zephyr Heights, a pop singer, and the pony behind Mane Melody. She turned a Maretime Bay storefront into a salon with a karaoke stage.

She lives at the Crystal Brighthouse with Sunny, Izzy, Zipp, and Misty. Jazz Hooves handles manes and nails at the salon; Rocky Riff handles hair. Her fans are the Pippsqueaks.

Pipp's Sparkle

Confidence takes practice.

It's the thing that gets a shy pony into the salon chair and the mic into their hoof. Some days it comes easy. Some days I'm still working on it.

In the story, I'm the element of confidence. That title is a lot to live up to on a bad mane day. I used to think confidence meant never being nervous. It doesn't. It means doing the thing anyway, with friends who cheer when you do. I still want every show to be perfect. It never is. The Pippsqueaks show up anyway.

“I'm a stylish pop star princess. My voice makes me strong. When my cutie mark comes to life, confidence is my song!”

Maretime Bay

The salon with a stage in it.

White brick outside, pink walls and a red carpet inside, and a stage shaped like a giant boombox.

I run it with Jazz Hooves and Rocky Riff. Book a mane, take the mic, or do both. Phyllis Cloverleaf had already closed five salons with one bad review. We gave her the mane she already had. She gave us five stars.
