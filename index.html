Who She Is

A pop star with a salon key.

Pipp Petals is the younger princess of Zephyr Heights, a pop star, and the pony behind Mane Melody. She turned a Maretime Bay storefront into a salon with a karaoke stage.

She grew up in the Royal Palace with Queen Haven and her older sister, Zipp. When magic came back, Pipp left the cloud city for the Crystal Brighthouse, where she lives with Sunny, Izzy, Zipp and later Misty.

Her fans are the Pippsqueaks. Her coworkers at Mane Melody are Jazz Hooves and Rocky Riff. Her cutie mark is a music note with a crown worked into it.

The Signature

Confidence is the job.

Confidence is the thing that gets a shy pony into the salon chair and the mic into their hoof.

Pipp has built a career on making that look easy. The stage, the salon, the livestream: same pony, same nerve.

“I'm a stylish pop star princess. My voice makes me strong. When my cutie mark comes to life, confidence is my song!”

Pipp Petals

Maretime Bay

The salon with a stage in it.

Mane Melody is white brick on the outside, pink walls and a red carpet on the inside, and a stage shaped like a giant boombox.

Pipp runs it with Jazz Hooves and Rocky Riff. Book a mane, take the mic, or do both. On a good night the whole room sings the chorus.
