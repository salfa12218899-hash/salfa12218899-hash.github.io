Pipp's Sparkle

Confidence takes practice.

It's the thing that gets a shy pony into the salon chair and the mic into their hoof. Some days it comes easy. Some days I'm still working on it.

Confidence is my sparkle. That is a lot to live up to on a bad mane day. I used to think confidence meant never being nervous. It doesn't. It means doing the thing anyway, with friends who cheer when you do. I still want every show to be perfect. It never is. The Pippsqueaks show up anyway.
